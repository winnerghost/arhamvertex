Crystal Depths

Mining Simulator

Mine glowing ore, sell it for coins and dig your way from the Stone Quarry down to the Magma Core.

  1. Geode Event

    Every 8 minutes a giant geode crashes into the hub. The whole server cracks it together.

  2. Shiny Ores

    1 in 150 rocks is shiny and worth 10×. Everyone sees when you find one.

  3. Helper Drones

    Drones fly beside you and mine on their own.

  4. Quests & Rebirth

    Three new quests a day, a 7-day streak, and a permanent coin bonus every time you start over.

Floor Ten

Escape the Tower

Ten floors. One way out. You wake in the lobby of an old, empty tower. Finish the task on each floor, unlock the stairs and reach the rescue helicopter on the roof.

  1. Flashlight

    Light your way, but watch the battery.

  2. Lockers

    Hide and hold your breath when something walks past.

  3. Sprint

    Run for it. Running makes noise.

  4. The Watcher

    Do not stare at it. The mannequins only move when nobody is looking.

  5. Together

    When anyone finishes a floor, the stairs open for everyone.

Clap to See

Sound is your only light

It is pitch black. Every sound you make lights the room for a moment. And something blind is listening.

  1. Clap

    Clap to see the room. Then move, because it heard that too.

  2. Four Keys

    Find 4 keys in the Grand Theatre and escape through the Stage Door.

  3. Stay Silent

    Sneak, and throw bottles to lure it away.

  4. Save Your Friends

    Play solo or with up to 3 friends.

( 03 ) About

I'm Arham. On Roblox, arhamvertex.

I'm an independent developer designing and scripting original experiences in Roblox Studio. I work across the whole game: the core loop, the systems underneath it, the world around it, and the moments players tell their friends about.

I'm drawn to two extremes. Horror built on tension you can hear, and simulators built on progress you can feel.
