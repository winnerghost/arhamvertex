    The floodline. Water rises in surges. The glowing line on the ground marks where the next surge will reach, and the tide pill at the top tells you when.

    Water hurts. Standing in floodwater drains health, and it drains faster later in the match. Armor does not protect you from it.

    The depth staff next to the minimap shows the water level, the next floodline and your own height. If your marker is orange, you are standing in ground that will flood.

    Steam vents launch you upward. Use them to reach high ground fast.
